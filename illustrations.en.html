🌊 The Video Blanking Challenge & Mathematical Phase Coherence

Mathematical Modeling

In CRT and digital displays, the electron beam / scanout controller pauses at the end of each line (Horizontal Blanking) and frame (Vertical Blanking) to return to the origin. During blanking intervals, the RAMDAC is physically clamped to 0 Volts. How do we ensure that radio carrier phase does not jump upon resuming active scanlines?

OUR POC APPROACH: ABSOLUTE PHASE COHERENCE
Prior Art Flaw

Without Mathematical Compensation

At the end of an active line, carrier phase is truncated. If the phase accumulator resets to zero or fails to account for unrendered blanking clock cycles, the waveform experiences a violent phase discontinuity.

Consequence: Severe out-of-band spectral splatter, audible periodic clicks, and complete failure of coherent demodulators (SSTV, SSB, PSK) to lock onto carrier phase.
Our Mathematical Breakthrough

Master float64 Accumulator across Virtual Raster

Phase advancement integrates all pixel clock cycles across virtual raster dimensions ($H_{\text{total}} \times V_{\text{total}}$), including unrendered blanking porches. Host CPU calculates master frame phase u_frame_phase in 64-bit float locked via swap_interval(1).

Result: Although output voltage is zero during blanking porches (0 V), the carrier resumes on the next line with exact continuous phase as if the transmission had never been interrupted!
